Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Qairo AI ("Qairo", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the Qairo AI assistant (the "Assistant"), an AI agent we operate that you reach through messaging channels such as WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and email, and that may connect to accounts you authorise, such as Google.

We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information we collect

  • Messages and content you send: text, voice notes, images, documents and other files you send to the Assistant, plus the Assistant's replies.
  • Contact and account details: your name, phone number, messaging-platform user ID, email address and any business details you give us.
  • Data from connected accounts: only when you explicitly authorise it. For example, with Google you may grant access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs or Contacts. We only access the services and scopes you approve.
  • Technical and usage data: delivery status, timestamps, error logs and similar operational records needed to run the service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Assistant's features you ask for, such as answering messages, reading and organising email, managing calendar events, preparing documents, sending reminders and running scheduled tasks.
  • To keep the service secure, reliable and working properly, including troubleshooting and preventing misuse.
  • To communicate with you about the service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Google user data

When you connect a Google account, the Assistant uses Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features you have asked for, such as reading, searching, drafting or sending email; viewing and managing calendar events; and reading or editing the Drive files, Sheets and Docs you direct it to.

Qairo AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, retargeting or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not sell Google user data or transfer it to data brokers or information resellers.
  • We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • Humans do not read Google user data unless you give us explicit permission for specific messages, it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, it is needed to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymised for internal operations.

Google user data is transferred only as needed to provide the features you request, for example to the AI model provider that processes your request (see section 4), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.

You can revoke the Assistant's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers we need to run the Assistant, and only as far as needed:

  • AI model providers (for example Anthropic and OpenAI), which process message content to generate responses. We use providers whose commercial API terms do not permit them to train their models on API data.
  • Messaging platforms (for example Meta/WhatsApp and Telegram) that carry messages between you and the Assistant.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that store and process data on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety or property of you, us or others. Some of these providers are located outside South Africa. Where information is transferred across borders, we rely on providers that offer adequate protection as POPIA requires.

5. Storage, security and retention

Information is stored on systems we control and with the service providers above. Access credentials, such as OAuth tokens, are stored with restricted file permissions and are never shared in chat. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, but no system is completely secure.

We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the service, or as required by law. When you stop using the Assistant or ask us to delete your data, we delete or anonymise it within a reasonable time. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information. You may also object to processing or withdraw consent, and you may disconnect any linked account at any time. To make a request, email bradleym@qairoai.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

7. Children

The Assistant is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. For significant changes, we will notify active users.

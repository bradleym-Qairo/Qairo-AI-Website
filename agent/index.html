Qairo AI Assistant

The Qairo AI Assistant is a Hermes-based AI agent used for authorised internal personal and business workflows. It is operated by Qairo AI in Johannesburg, South Africa, with Bradley Mann as the contact. It is not a public sign-up service or the official Hermes Agent project.

What the assistant does

The assistant helps authorised users research questions, organise information, prepare drafts, manage reminders and carry out approved workflows. It can be used through a local interface or configured messaging channels.

Google account connections

When an authorised user connects a Google account, the assistant can use the permissions granted to help with email, calendar events, documents, spreadsheets, files and contacts. Available actions depend on the services configured and permissions approved by that user. This page does not grant access to any account.

Account access is used for requested assistant features, not advertising or selling Google user data. Review the Privacy Policy for how information is accessed, used, stored and shared, including AI processing. You can revoke Google access at Google Account permissions.

Local operation and AI processing

The agent is locally operated, but that does not mean all processing stays on the device. Connected Google services, configured AI model providers and other enabled integrations may process information required for a task. AI output can be wrong; important outputs and actions require human review.

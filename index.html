FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

What exactly is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that can understand context, use approved tools and follow a defined workflow to move work forward. Unlike a generic chatbot, it is built around your business process, connected only to the systems it needs, and designed with clear boundaries and human approvals where they matter.

Is my data safe?

Yes — and this is treated as the most important part of the setup, not an afterthought. Your assistant runs on your own accounts and infrastructure, access is scoped to only what it needs, and your knowledge base belongs to you. Nothing is pooled with other clients, and nothing is used to train public models. If you ever leave, you keep everything.

How long does setup take?

A focused first workflow can usually be live and useful within the first few weeks. More complex deployments depend on the number of systems, data quality and the approvals required. We start where value is clearest, then expand deliberately.

What does it cost?

Pricing is a once-off discovery and implementation fee, followed by an ongoing operating retainer where needed. It depends on the workflow, integrations, data and support required. If a system cannot save meaningful time, reduce risk or improve service, it is not the right project to build.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Qairo handles the technical design, setup, integrations and maintenance. Your team works with agents through the interfaces that make sense for the job — including WhatsApp, email, chat tools or the systems already in use — without needing to become AI specialists.

Will agents replace my team?

No. The goal is to remove repetitive administration and give capable people more leverage — not to hand the business over to an uncontrolled machine. Qairo designs clear approval points, access limits and escalation paths so people remain accountable for the decisions that need judgement.

Where is work getting stuck?

No pitch, no pressure. Send a short note about the process, backlog or customer experience that is eating your team’s time. Brad will tell you honestly whether an agent system is a fit — and where to start.
